# Milvus Meme Sticker Style Guide

## Purpose

Make small original stickers that create emotional resonance in technical articles and community posts. The sticker should interrupt dense technical prose with a recognizable feeling, not explain the architecture.

## Character

- Use an abstract Milvus-inspired bird/eagle mascot.
- Keep it cute, simplified, and expressive.
- Do not copy a logo exactly.
- Do not make the entire character blue. Use light blue as the main accent, with white, cream, yellow/orange beak, dark eyes, and small colorful props.

## Sticker Feel

- Square composition.
- Thick clean outline.
- White sticker border.
- Hand-drawn or doodle-like finish.
- Big facial expression.
- Simple background, often off-white or transparent-looking.
- Compact shape that remains readable when small.

## Good Scenes

- Buried under API docs, config files, schemas, and database blocks.
- Tangled in connector cables, but keep the face and mouth readable.
- Staring at mismatched sources: database, chat, file, code, ticket, cloud bucket.
- Melting from too many sync jobs.
- Relieved after one unified interface replaces many tools.

## Avoid

- Text, captions, speech bubbles with words, labels, fake code, or readable UI strings.
- All-blue character treatment, especially blue teeth or blue mouth interiors.
- Existing meme templates such as panda-head, reaction screenshots, celebrity faces, anime frames, or movie stills.
- Overly polished SaaS illustration, photorealism, 3D toy style, or detailed corporate mascot rendering.
- Props so dense that the face cannot be read at 240 px.

## Variation Pattern

When asked for options, produce three variants:

1. Closest to the paragraph's literal pain point.
2. Most meme-like and emotionally exaggerated.
3. Cleanest technical-marketing version with fewer props.
